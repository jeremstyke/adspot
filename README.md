## How it works

1. Open [@PostOnAdSpotBot](https://t.me/PostOnAdSpotBot?start=src_github) and tap **Post my ad**.
2. Fill in your ad: title, text, image, link and category. Let the AI polish it in one tap.
3. After a quick check, your ad is posted on [@adspotchannel](https://t.me/adspotchannel) and on the partner channel you picked.

Need more visibility? A second ad on the same day costs 50 Telegram Stars. The first one is always free.

## For channel owners: free subscribers

Add AdSpot to your channel in 1 click and become a partner:

- ✅ Your channel shows **1 to 3 ads a day**, spaced out automatically
- ✅ Every advertiser who picks your channel **must join it**
- ✅ Ads are **deleted after 24 hours**
- ✅ Change the settings or stop anytime

## About this repository

This repository holds the AdSpot website. `build.js` generates one home page and one partner page per language from `i18n/*.json`, and GitHub Actions publishes it on GitHub Pages. To add a language, drop a new JSON file in `i18n/` with the same keys as `en.json`.
