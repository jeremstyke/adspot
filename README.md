# AdSpot 📢 Free ads on Telegram

**AdSpot** lets anyone promote a Telegram channel, group, bot, mini app or website for free.

👉 **Post your ad:** [@PostOnAdSpotBot](https://t.me/PostOnAdSpotBot?start=src_github)
📣 **See the ads:** [@adspotchannel](https://t.me/adspotchannel)
🌐 **Website:** https://jeremstyke.github.io/adspot/

## How it works

1. Open [@PostOnAdSpotBot](https://t.me/PostOnAdSpotBot?start=src_github) and tap **Post my ad**.
2. Fill in your ad in the mini app: title, text, image (optional), link, category and hashtags. A live preview shows exactly how it will look.
3. Every ad is checked by a human, then posted on [@adspotchannel](https://t.me/adspotchannel) with a button to your link.

- ✅ 1 free ad every day
- ✨ Write in your own language: the AI translates your ad into English and can improve it for free
- 🏷 Hashtags suggested by the AI
- 📣 Your ad can also go on a partner channel of the same topic (you just have to join it)

## Partner channels 🤝

Own a Telegram channel? Become a partner in 1 click:

👉 [Add AdSpot to my channel](https://t.me/PostOnAdSpotBot?startchannel&admin=post_messages+edit_messages+delete_messages)

- Your channel shows 1 to 3 ads per day, spaced through the day and deleted after 24 hours.
- Every advertiser who picks your channel must join it: **free subscribers for you**.
- Choose the topics you accept, the language and the number of ads, or stop anytime by removing the bot.

## Rules

No scams, no adult content, no misleading ads. Every ad is reviewed before it is published.
